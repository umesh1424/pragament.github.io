Traditional smart-classroom setups often require a dedicated projector room or a TV in every classroom. Both options can be costly and limit access to digital content across the school day.

**TV on Wheels** is a practical alternative. A TV mounted on a mobile stand can be moved between classrooms, giving teachers flexible access to digital lessons without major infrastructure investments.

## How It Works
- Teachers can use a USB drive to play educational videos and presentations.
- Phones or tablets can be cast to the TV for interactive app-based learning.
- A connected computer or media player can run lessons, quizzes, and multimedia content.

## Why Schools Choose It
- Increases access to digital learning across multiple classrooms
- Reduces idle hardware by sharing one high-quality setup
- Supports interactive, student-centered lessons
