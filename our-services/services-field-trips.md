# Field Trips to Research Facilities

We organize educational field trips to real research labs and innovation centers so students can see science and technology in action.

---

## What Students Gain
- Exposure to real-world research and careers
- Inspiration to pursue STEM pathways
- Context for classroom learning

---

## Our Approach
We bridge gaps in access and opportunity by connecting students with experiences they may not otherwise receive. We also identify and assist groups of students, ensuring targeted and impactful support.
