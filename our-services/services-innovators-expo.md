# Innovators’ Expo (Digital Carnival)

Innovators’ Expo is a school-wide technology showcase where students present creative projects to parents, teachers, and visitors from other schools. It builds school reputation while giving students a joyful stage to grow.

---

## What We Provide
- End-to-end guidance for planning and execution
- Project ideas using Python, AI, Robotics, and IoT
- Support for teachers, students, and school leadership

---

## Who Can Participate
- Grades 1 to 10
- Individual or team projects
- Projects aligned to real-world problems

---

## Flexible Scheduling
Schools can conduct Innovators’ Expo annually at any time in the academic year.

---

## Impact
Students present their work with confidence, improve communication skills, and inspire a school culture that celebrates creativity and innovation.
