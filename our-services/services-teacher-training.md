# Teacher Training

Pragament trains teachers to deliver 21st-century computer science education confidently and effectively.

---

## What We Cover
- Modern CS curriculum aligned with CBSE and State boards
- Teacher guides, lesson plans, worksheets, and activities
- Classroom-ready projects and assessments

---

## Outcomes
- Teachers become confident facilitators of coding and STEM learning
- Students transition from users to creators of technology
- Schools build sustainable tech education programs
