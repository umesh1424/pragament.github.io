# Coding, Robotics, AI, IoT & Math Workshops

Pragament runs joyful, hands-on workshops that make STEM learning practical and exciting. Our workshops combine Coding, Robotics, AI, IoT, and Math with English communication skills (reading, writing, speaking), value education, and leadership.

---

## What Students Learn
- Visual Coding, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- AI, IoT, Robotics, and Game Development
- Problem-solving, logic, patience, and teamwork

---

## Project-Based Learning for All Levels
Pragament has an extensive pool of projects for Websites, Web Apps, AI, IoT, Robotics, and Game Development. Every workshop includes a complete teacher guide that is easily accessible on our platform.

- Designed for ages 4 to 16
- Beginner, intermediate, and advanced tracks
- Multiple project ideas for elementary, middle, and high school students
- Encourages participation in school and global coding competitions

---

## Why It Works
We bridge gaps in access and opportunity by providing equitable learning experiences and joyful STEM learning that builds confidence and curiosity.
