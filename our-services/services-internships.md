# Student Internships with On-Campus Startups

Our internship program places students in real startup environments so they can learn by doing and build future-ready skills.

---

## What Students Experience
- Hands-on work in a real startup workplace
- Scientific competencies that prepare them for a college major and career of their choice
- Team collaboration and leadership development

---

## Holistic Impact
- Enhanced academic performance through better focus and confidence
- Stronger communication and problem-solving abilities
- Growth in responsibility and initiative

---

## Equity & Access
We connect skilled individuals with those in need, bridging gaps and providing resources where they are most needed.
